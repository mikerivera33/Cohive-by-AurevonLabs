Privacy Policy — Cohive by AurevonLabs

Effective date: September 6, 2026 Controller: AurevonLabs (“we,” “us”) Product: Cohive Contact: privacy@aurevonlabs.com

> Template for product launch. Have counsel review for GDPR/CCPA and your actual data flows before production reliance.

1. What we collect

- Account data: name, email, phone (if provided), auth provider IDs, password hashes (if password auth). - Billing data: handled primarily by Stripe (card details are not stored on Cohive servers). We store Stripe customer/subscription/price IDs and plan tier. - App data: trips, votes, members, imported text (sanitized), preferences, referral codes. - Technical data: IP address, device/browser type, approximate logs for security and rate limiting. - Cookies / local storage: session tokens and UI preferences on your device.

2. How we use data

To provide Cohive, authenticate users, process payments, prevent abuse, improve the product, communicate service notices, and comply with law.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Contract performance, legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent where required, and legal obligation.

4. Sharing

We share data with processors such as Stripe (payments), hosting providers (e.g. Netlify/Vercel), and OAuth providers you choose (Google/Apple). We do not sell personal information. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

We retain account and trip data while your account is active and as needed for legal, tax, and dispute purposes. You may request deletion subject to lawful retention needs.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, hashed passwords, access controls). No method is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or object to certain processing. Contact privacy@aurevonlabs.com. California residents may have CCPA rights including “Do Not Sell/Share” (we do not sell).

8. Children

Cohive is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect their data.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries with appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in-product when required.
