# Privacy Policy — Cohive by AurevonLabs

**Effective date:** September 17, 2026  
**Controller:** AurevonLabs ("we", "us")  
**Product:** Cohive  
**Contact:** privacy@aurevonlabs.com

> Template for product launch. Have counsel review for GDPR / CCPA and your actual data flows before relying on it in production.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, phone number if you provide one, the stable account identifier your sign-in provider (Google or Apple) gives us, and a password hash if you sign in with a password.  
- **Payout handle:** if you add one, the Venmo, Cash App or PayPal username you want hive members to pay you at. It is shown only to members of your hives.  
- **Billing data:** purchases are processed by Apple, Google or Stripe; card details never reach Cohive servers. Through RevenueCat we store your subscription status, the product or price identifiers you bought, and the processor's customer identifier.  
- **App data:** hives, trips, places, votes, itineraries, expenses, pot contributions and settlements, saved homes and restaurants, imported text (sanitized), preferences and referral codes.  
- **Technical data:** IP address, device and browser type, and short-lived logs used for security and rate limiting.  
- **Cookies and local storage:** session cookies, a short-lived sign-in handoff cookie, and interface preferences kept on your device.

## 2. How we use data

To provide Cohive, sign you in, keep hive members in sync, process purchases, pay referral commissions, prevent abuse, improve the product, send service notices (including sign-in links) and comply with law.

## 3. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Contract performance, legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent where required, and legal obligation.

## 4. Sharing

Members of a hive see what is shared inside it: names, places, votes, expenses, pot balances and payout handles. Beyond that we share data only with processors that run the Service: Apple, Google and Stripe (purchases), RevenueCat (subscription status), Resend (transactional email), our hosting and database providers, map tile providers (which receive the map areas you view), and the sign-in provider you choose. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## 5. Retention

We keep account and hive data while your account is active and as long as needed for legal, tax and dispute purposes. When you delete your account, your profile is erased and your sign-ins are revoked; shared ledgers keep an anonymous "Deleted member" placeholder so other members' balances still add up.

## 6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: HTTPS, hashed passwords, HttpOnly session cookies, provider-verified sign-in, server-enforced access control and rate limiting. No method is completely secure.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export or object to certain processing. You can delete your account from the You tab at any time, or contact privacy@aurevonlabs.com. California residents may have CCPA rights, including "Do Not Sell or Share" (we do not sell or share for advertising).

## 8. Children

Cohive is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect their data.

## 9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and other countries with appropriate safeguards.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app when required.
